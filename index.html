For families who look after a parent's health

Understand your health reports in simple English.

Add a blood test or scan report. FollowThrough Health explains every result in plain words, shows which ones to ask about first, and gets you ready for the doctor. Free, and read on your own device.

What happens to my data?
  • Explaining a report: the PDF, photo or numbers are read on this phone or computer. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Only if you choose to save it: signing in and tapping "Save to my account" stores the report and its results on our servers, so you can see them on other devices. You can delete them any time.
  • Only if you share it: "Share with my doctor" stores the summary you chose, encrypted, behind a private link you can turn off.
  • Only if you use the help chat: your question goes to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. Our servers do not save it.
  • Always: anonymous daily counts such as "a report was explained", with no report text and no account. We never sell your data or use it for ads.

The moment we fix

A report lands at 9 pm. Now what?

  1. Abbreviations and ranges written for clinicians. FollowThrough Health explains every line in plain words.

  2. Each result is checked against the lab's own range. Results outside it are ones to talk to your doctor about; how far outside does not decide urgency. A level labs often treat as urgent, or emergency symptoms written in the report, gets a contact-your-doctor-today or emergency-care message.

  3. Questions written from your numbers, and a short one-page brief for your doctor.

Safe by design

Education that sends you to your doctor, never around them.

FollowThrough Health does

  • Compare each result with the lab's own range
  • Say "contact your doctor today" for dangerous levels
  • Ask you to check numbers read from a photo
  • Label general guides honestly when no range is printed

FollowThrough Health never

  • Diagnoses
  • Names medicines or doses
  • Reads scan images (only the radiologist's words)
  • Replaces your doctor

FollowThrough Health is not a diagnosis, prescription, emergency service, or replacement for licensed medical care. If you feel very unwell, call your local emergency number.
