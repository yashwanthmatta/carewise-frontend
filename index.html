What happens to my data?
  • Explaining a report: the PDF, photo or numbers are read on this phone or computer. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Only if you choose to save it: signing in and tapping "Save to my account" stores the report and its results on our servers, so you can see them on other devices. You can delete them any time.
  • Only if you share it: "Share with my doctor" stores the summary you chose, encrypted, behind a private link you can turn off.
  • Only if you use the help chat: your question goes to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the answer. Our servers do not save it.
  • Always: anonymous daily counts such as "a report was explained", with no report text and no account. We never sell your data or use it for ads.
