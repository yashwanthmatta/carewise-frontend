The moment we fix

A report lands at 9 pm. Now what?

  1. Abbreviations and ranges written for clinicians. CareWise explains every line in plain words.

  2. Each result is checked against the lab's own range. Far outside means ask soon; a dangerous level means contact your doctor today.

  3. Questions written from your numbers, and a one-page brief your doctor can read in 30 seconds.

Safe by design

Education that sends you to your doctor, never around them.

CareWise does

  • Compare each result with the lab's own range
  • Say "contact your doctor today" for dangerous levels
  • Ask you to check numbers read from a photo
  • Label general guides honestly when no range is printed

CareWise never

  • Diagnoses
  • Names medicines or doses
  • Reads scan images (only the radiologist's words)
  • Replaces your doctor

CareWise is not a diagnosis, prescription, emergency service, or replacement for licensed medical care. If you feel very unwell, call your local emergency number.
