How it will work

Five steps, with a person at the important one.

  1. Choose a test. Read what it measures and how to prepare, from the lab's own instructions.
  2. A licensed clinician reviews the request. They can approve it, decline it or ask you a question. Paying never approves a test on its own.
  3. Visit a partner lab. At a time the lab has confirmed, with your booking reference.
  4. Results come straight from the lab. Every value, unit and range exactly as the lab reports them, with any later corrections kept alongside.
  5. Read your clinician's summary and the plain-language explanation. The clinician's signed note is always marked apart from anything written by AI.

Questions

About lab testing

Can I order a test today?

No. Lab testing is planned and not available yet. You can still upload a report you already have and get it explained, for free.

Which labs will you work with?

We have no lab partners yet. We will only name a lab once it has signed an agreement with us, and only show locations we have checked.

Who reviews the results?

The plan is for a licensed clinician to review each request and sign a summary of your results. Anything written by AI is labelled as such and never presented as a clinician's note.

What will it cost?

Prices will be set with partner labs and shown before you pay. Explaining reports you upload stays free.

What happens to my information?

Only what the lab and the clinician need to carry out your test, under agreements that protect it, and only with your consent. See the privacy policy.

What if a result needs urgent attention?

Before launch, the lab and the clinical partner will agree on how urgent results reach you quickly by phone. An app message or the AI chat will never be the only way you hear. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Lab testing is planned and not available. FollowThrough Health explains reports for education; it does not diagnose and does not replace your doctor. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
