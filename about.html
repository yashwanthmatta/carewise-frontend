About

Helping families follow through

Most people get lab results as a page of numbers, alone, days before they can ask anyone. FollowThrough Health turns that page into plain words, the right questions, and a reminder of what comes next.

Why we built it

Caregivers often sit between a parent's doctor and a stack of reports. They need to know what matters, how soon to ask, and what to bring to the visit. We built FollowThrough Health for that moment: understand the report, know what to ask, and remember the next step.

What we believe

  • Plain words. Every result explained the way you would explain it to family.
  • Your doctor decides. We prepare you for the conversation; we never replace it.
  • Private by default. Reports are read on your device, and we never sell data.
  • Honest limits. We say what we test and what we have not proven yet. See Safety and trust.

Where we are

FollowThrough Health is an early-stage product. The website is free to use, with phone apps on the way. We are looking for families and clinicians to help shape it.

Try it with a sample report

FollowThrough Health explains reports for education. It does not diagnose and does not replace your doctor. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
