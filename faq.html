FAQ

Questions people ask

Short answers. If yours is not here, see Support.

Which reports can it read?

Blood tests (blood count, sugar, cholesterol, kidney, liver, thyroid, vitamins, iron and more) and the written part of CT, MRI and X-ray reports. Add a PDF, a photo, or type the numbers. It reads US, UK, European and Indian formats and units.

Is it a doctor or a diagnosis?

No. It explains the report in plain words and helps you prepare for your doctor. It does not diagnose, treat or replace medical care.

What does it cost?

Explaining reports, the doctor brief and the health record are free. Plus is $7 a month (personal plan, reminders, trends). Family is $12 a month for up to 5 people. Cancel any time.

Do I need an account?

No. Reports are explained on your device without one. Create a free account only to keep reports across devices or share a link with your doctor.

Is my report uploaded?

Not unless you choose to save or share it. See Safety and trust for exactly what leaves your device.

How accurate is it?

On our test set of 200 results it reads every value and flag correctly, but no reader is perfect, especially with photos. Always check the original report and talk to your doctor.

Can I use it for my mom or dad?

Yes. That is who it is built for. Choose whose report it is, keep a record for each family member, and bring the doctor brief to their visit.

Which languages?

English and Spanish. The Spanish wording is a draft still being reviewed.

How do I delete my data?

Profile, then "Delete my account". It deletes everything saved to your account straight away. Full steps.

I am a doctor. Can my clinic use it?

DocBridge lets a clinician approve a plain-English explanation for each patient. It is in a pilot with sample or de-identified labs. Learn more.

FollowThrough Health explains reports for education. It does not diagnose and does not replace your doctor. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
