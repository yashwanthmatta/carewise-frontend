# Rules 2026-10-20.7: photo ranges that lost their decimal point

The protected tag `clinical-review-candidate-2026-10-20.2` and the .5 review pack in
`docs/clinical-validation-2026-10-20.5/` are unchanged. **This change is not clinically
validated.** It needs the same clinician review as .3 to .6 before it counts as validated.

Version .7 holds three extraction safety fixes for reports read from a photo or scan on the
device. No threshold, wording of an explanation, or rule for typed or PDF text changed.

## What went wrong

The photo reader (Tesseract) can drop a decimal point from the printed range, not only from
the result. Before .7 only about 25 common tests had a check for that. For any other test the
misread was shown as if it were right:

| Line on the report | Read from the photo | .6 showed | .7 does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Lymphocytes 2.5 x10^9/L 1.5-4.0` | `... 2.5 x10^9/L 15-40` | low | asks: "Did the report say 2.5 x10^9/L 1.5-4.0?" |
| `Neutrophils 8.6 x10^9/L 2.0-7.5` | `... 8.6 x10^9/L 20-75` | low (truly high) | asks with the range fixed |
| `Neutrophils 9,3 x10^9/L 2,0-7,5` | `... 9,3 x10^9/L 2,0-75` | normal (truly high) | asks, keeping the report's decimal comma |
| `Protein, Total 9.4 g/dL 6.0-8.5` | `... 94H g/dL 6.0-85` | 94, high | asks: "Did the report say 9.4H g/dL 6.0-8.5?" |
| `Prothrombin Time 10.5 sec 9.4-12.5` | `... 105 sec 9.4-125` | 105, normal | asks with both fixed |

## The rule

Only for text read from a photo. For a line with a result and a printed range:
- one side of the range has a decimal mark and the other lost it ("6.0-85"), or
- the result has a decimal mark and the whole range lost it ("2.5 ... 15-40").

If putting the point back makes the result fit its range, and the reading changes, the person
is asked to compare the line with the report. Nothing is changed until they choose. While a
line is waiting, that result counts as "still to confirm", as for the other photo checks.
Lines that already fit (`Hemoglobin 13.5 g/dL 12-16`, `WBC 6.2 4.0-11`, `CRP 0.5 0.0-10`) are
not asked about. Tests with their own photo rule (magnesium, potassium and others) keep it.

Confirming a suggestion replaces the whole "result ... range" part of the line, so the
replacement now escapes every special character (it used to escape only the first ".").

## Two more cases (same version)

**A result and its range that both lost the point.** "Lymphocytes 4.8 x10^9/L 1.5-4.0" read as
"48 ... 15-40" looks consistent. For six tests, in units where ten times the usual range is never a
real range, a printed range about ten times the usual adult range is asked about:

| Test | Units it applies to | Usual range used to spot the slip |
|---|---|---|
| Lymphocytes, neutrophils, monocytes | absolute counts (x10^9/L, /uL), not % | 1.0-4.0, 1.8-7.7, 0.2-1.0 |
| Glucose, urea | mmol/L | 3.9-6.1, 2.5-7.8 |
| A1C | % | 4.0-6.0 |

These usual ranges are only used to decide whether to ask. They never decide whether a result is
high or low; the printed range does. `Lymphocytes 30 % 20-40` and `HbA1c 42 mmol/mol 20-42` are
not asked about.

**Reports printed in two columns.** Only the first test on a line was checked. Each column is now
checked on its own (the same column split the reader already uses), and confirming a suggestion
changes only that column.

## Evidence

- `scripts/eval/ocr-range-decimals.mjs` (in CI): 10 misread lines asked about or fixed, 12 correct
  lines left alone, a two-column line checked in its second column, magnesium still fixed by its
  own rule, and confirming a suggestion fixes the line.
- Photo stress test, 60 synthetic reports each, results shown with a wrong number and no question:

| Report set | Rules .6 (main) | Rules .7 |
|---|---|---|
| seed 7 (used while building) | 38 of 629 | 28 |
| seed 11 (new set, checked after) | 39 of 631 | 24 |

  Wrong high/low: 13 → 9 and 11 → 7. Correct "X of N outside range" line: 77% → 82% and 72% → 78%.
  Results the person is asked to confirm: 11% → 14%. Critical values handled urgently: 100% in all
  runs. Medicine names: 0.
- Text stress test, 2,000 synthetic reports (typed and PDF text): unchanged at 100%.

## Still open (not fixed here)

- A range whose dash was lost ("2.5-7.0" read as "257.0") leaves the result without a range.
- Limits printed as "<1.0" or ">1.0" (for example HDL in mmol/L) that lost their point.
- About 1 in 23 photo results is still shown with a wrong number and no question. A typed or
  PDF report avoids photo reading altogether.
