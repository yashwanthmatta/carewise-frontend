# Rules 2026-10-20.8: photo ranges that lost a dash or two decimals

The protected tag `clinical-review-candidate-2026-10-20.2` and the .5 review pack in
`docs/clinical-validation-2026-10-20.5/` are unchanged. **This change is not clinically
validated.** It needs the same clinician review as .3 to .7 before it counts as validated.

Version .8 holds three extraction safety fixes for reports read from a photo or scan on the
device. No threshold, wording of an explanation, or rule for typed or PDF text changed.

## What went wrong

| Line on the report | Read from the photo | .7 showed | .8 does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RBC 5.97 x10E6/uL 4.14-5.80` | `... 414-580` | **low** (truly high) | asks: "Did the report say 5.97 x10E6/uL 4.14-5.80?" |
| `RBC 4,79 x10E6/uL 4,14-5,80` | `RBC 4,79 ... 414-580` | turned 4,79 into "4,7.9", low | leaves 4,79 alone, asks with the range fixed (decimal comma kept) |
| `Uric Acid 3.4 mg/dL 2.5-7.0` | `... 257.0` | no range | asks: "Did the report say 2.5-7.0?" |
| `Urea 6.0 mmol/L 2.5-7.8` | `... 2578` | no range | asks with 2.5-7.8 |
| `INR 0.9 0.8-1.2` | `INR 0.9 0.81.2` | no range | asks with 0.8-1.2 |
| `AST 21 U/L 10-35` | `AST 21 U/L 1035` | no range | asks with 10-35 |

## The rules

Only for text read from a photo. Nothing is changed until the person chooses.

1. **Both decimals lost.** When the result has two decimals and the whole range has none,
   the point goes back with the same number of decimals as the result. Before, only one
   decimal was tried, so "414-580" could never become "4.14-5.80".
2. **Dash lost.** When a result has no range, and the last number after it (not part of a unit,
   a date, or an earlier result with its own unit) can be split only one way into a range that:
   has the result's own number of decimals, has low below high, and has the result near it,
   the person is asked whether that was the range. If there are two possible splits, nothing is asked.
3. **Decimal comma.** The photo check for about 25 common tests read the "79" of "4,79" as a
   separate number and "fixed" it to 7.9. It now skips digits after a decimal comma.

A result fixed by its own photo rule (uric acid 34 → 3.4) still gets the question about its range.

Lines that are not asked about: `RBC 4.79 x10E6/uL 4.14-5.80`, `eGFR 41 mL/min/1.73m2`,
`Platelets 250 x10^3/uL`, `Glucose 105 98 01/02/2026 mg/dL`, `Cholesterol 210 198 mg/dL`,
`Glucose 105 mg/dL 2026`.

## Evidence

- `scripts/eval/ocr-range-decimals.mjs` (in CI): 11 new misread lines asked about, 7 new correct
  lines left alone, and confirming a "lost dash" suggestion gives the line its range back.
- Photo stress test, 60 synthetic reports each:

| Report set | Measure | Rules .7 | Rules .8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| seed 7 | Wrong high/low | 9 | 0 |
| seed 7 | Wrong number, no question | 28 | 27 |
| seed 7 | Correct "X of N outside range" line | 82% | 85% |
| seed 11 | Wrong high/low | 7 | 1 |
| seed 11 | Wrong number, no question | 24 | 22 |
| seed 11 | Correct "X of N outside range" line | 78% | 80% |

  Results the person is asked to confirm: about 14% → 16%. Critical values handled urgently:
  100%. Medicine names: 0.
- Text stress test, 2,000 synthetic reports: unchanged at 100%.

## Still open

- Limits printed as "<1.0" or ">1.0" (HDL in mmol/L) that lost their point ("1.2" read as "12").
- eGFR with an extra digit ("52" read as "521").
- About 1 in 25 photo results is still shown with a wrong number and no question.
- The phone app sends photos to the server, which reads them another way. That path does not
  have these checks yet.
