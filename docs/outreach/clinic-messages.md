# DocBridge clinic outreach: messages ready to send

Sendable text only. The founder sends these personally, one at a time, to a named person. Fill in the
[brackets]. `scripts/claims_audit.mjs` checks this file in CI, so a banned claim here fails the build.
How to use them, the call script and the pricing questions are in `docs/clinic-outreach-kit.md`.

---

## 1. First email (cold, to a clinician or practice manager)

**Subject:** A plain-language lab explanation your patients can read, approved by you

Hi Dr. [Last name],

I'm [your name], founder of FollowThrough Health. We're building DocBridge for small practices: after
a lab result comes back, it drafts a plain-language explanation, a clinician edits and approves it,
and the patient gets a private link. Nothing reaches a patient without a clinician's approval.

We're early. It's in an evaluation with made-up clinics and sample labs only, and an independent
clinical review is under way. I'm looking for three practices to tell me honestly whether this would
be useful, and what it would be worth to them.

You can try it in two minutes with a made-up clinic and sample labs, no sign-in:
https://followthroughhealth.com/#doctor-demo

Would you have 20 minutes in the next two weeks for a call? I'd mostly ask how your practice
explains results today.

Thank you,
[Your name]
FollowThrough Health · [postal address]
If you'd rather not hear from me again, reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.
