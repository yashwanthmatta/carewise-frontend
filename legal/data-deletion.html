Data Deletion

FollowThrough Health Data Deletion Request

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Delete your account yourself

You can delete your FollowThrough Health account and the data saved with it at any time. It happens straight away and cannot be undone.

  • Phone app: open Account and press "Delete my account". The same button is also under Legal, in Account privacy controls.
  • Website: sign in at followthroughhealth.com, open Profile, open "Log out, verify email, reset password or delete account" and press "Delete my account".

If you pay for Plus or Family, cancel the plan first from "Manage or cancel plan". Deleting the account does not cancel a store or card subscription by itself.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in sessions and password reset or verification codes.
  • Profiles for you and your family members, health record entries, medicines, saved reports and their files, and lab trends.
  • Medicine reminders and their phone numbers, and doctor share links.
  • Consent records, notification settings, plan records, and the activity log tied to your account.
  • If you own a DocBridge clinic: the clinic, its team, its lab drafts and their files.

What may be kept

  • A short record that a deletion happened (the date and a request number), so we can show the request was completed.
  • Payment records that the law requires Stripe or the app stores to keep. These hold no health information.
  • Server backups, if any exist, until they are overwritten on their normal schedule.
  • Reports you kept only on your own phone or browser. Remove them with "Clear history", or by removing the app.

If you cannot sign in

Reset your password from Profile first. If that does not work, use the "Was this helpful?" box on any result to reach the team, give the email address on your account and say you want it deleted. Do not include health details. We will confirm the email belongs to you before deleting anything.
