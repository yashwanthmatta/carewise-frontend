FollowThrough Health Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Educational Use Only

FollowThrough Health provides educational summaries and care navigation support. It is not a medical device, clinical diagnosis tool, prescription system, or emergency service.

Not Medical Advice

FollowThrough Health does not give medical advice and does not create a doctor-patient relationship. Do not start, stop or change any medicine or treatment because of something FollowThrough Health says. Talk to your doctor first.

Professional Review Required

Users should review FollowThrough Health output with licensed healthcare professionals, especially for symptoms, abnormal reports, medications, pregnancy, chronic conditions, children, older adults, or high-risk cases.

AI Can Be Wrong

Explanations, including AI-written ones, can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. Numbers read from a photo can be misread, so always check them against the original report. FollowThrough Health is not 100% accurate, and it does not have a doctor review your report unless your own clinic sends you an explanation it approved.

Emergency Warning

If a user has severe symptoms, chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs, severe allergic reaction, suicidal thoughts, uncontrolled bleeding, or other urgent concerns, they should call 911 (or their local emergency number) or go to the nearest emergency room now.
